# Tax Refund vs Owe Estimator

Enter your gross annual income, filing status, total withholding, deductions, and tax credits to get an estimate of your US federal tax refund or balance due using 2024 tax brackets. The app shows a full breakdown from gross income to taxable income, displays marginal and effective tax rates, details tax by bracket, and offers a practical W-4 adjustment suggestion. Inputs are saved locally so you can revisit your estimate.
